Section 6 - Storage and Backup During the Research Process

Q6a. During Research Data Storage:
Local storage and local backup for fully anonymised national audit data. University (Azure/OneDrive) use back up for qualitative data.
Q6b. During Research Data Security:
Stroke audit data will be fully anonymised and will not be identifyable. For qualitative (interview and workshop) data: All paper data, such as consent forms and diversity monitoring forms (where applicable), will be kept in locked storage until digital copies have been created, at which point the paper versions will be destroyed in university confidential waste. Consent forms and diversity monitoring will be stored separately from research data and will not be linked. Diversity monitoring be fully anonymous. All electronic data, including the recordings from the interview, will be stored on Northumbria University computer systems, which are password protected. All data will be stored in accordance with Northumbria University policy, Data Protection Act (2018) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Personal identifiable information such as contact details will be destroyed when the study ends. All written and recorded information, such as anonymised interview transcripts will be destroyed seven years after the study ends. Personal data will be stored electronically on password protected computers at Northumbria University only, where the principal investigator (PI) and study researchers are based. Personal data, such as names and contact details, will only be stored on University servers. When working remotely the research team can access the university servers via Microsoft OneDrive or via shared documents on Microsoft Teams. Both solutions mean that local versions are not required on laptops. Personal data contained within consent forms (audio or digital) will also be stored electronically. Where consent is taken face-to-face, paper consent forms will be scanned and stored electronically on University servers then destroyed. Members of the research team not based at the host institution will not need to have access to personal data of participants. Interviews and focus groups will be transcribed, with the transcriptions anonymised prior to data analysis. An external transcription company will be used to transcribe interviews. A confidentiality and non-disclosure statement will be signed by the transcription company to ensure confidentiality. We will only collect and store personal data that is required for the conduct of the study, such as name, telephone number and email addresses of participants. The legal basis for processing the personal data required for the purposes of this study is that the research is being conducted in the public interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(e)) Members of the research team will ensure that all personal data will remain confidential. The only exception to this is in relation to focus groups. As co-design workshops will have other people present, we cannot guarantee confidentiality or anonymity but we will encourage all participants not to discuss any potentially sensitive topics afterwards (though we do not anticipate any sensitive topic arising). Participants will be informed of this in advance.
